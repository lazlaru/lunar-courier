Privacy Policy

Lunar Courier · Effective October 30, 2026

Lunar Courier is a game created by LD Clinton. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the Lunar Courier app for Mac.

Information we collect

None. Lunar Courier does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services in the game.

Data saved on your device

The game saves your story progress, best scores and sound settings on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device, and nobody else can see it. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the Lunar Courier support page.
