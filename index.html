COURIER

The Moon's colonies run on deliveries, and you're the courier. Strap on a jetpack, thread each parcel through the right dome's glowing ring, and dodge drones, maglev trains and solar flares along the way.

FEATURES

  • Story mode: ten shifts, from your first day on the job to the final delivery at Artemis Port
  • Endless mode: fly as far as you can as the Moon gets faster and busier
  • Hand-crafted procedural graphics and an original synthesized soundtrack
  • Works fully offline, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking
