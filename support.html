Lunar Courier Support

Help for the Lunar Courier app for Mac

Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and include your Mac model and macOS version. You'll get a reply there.

How to play

Fly
Hold Space, ↑ or the mouse button to fire your thrusters.
Deliver
The card at the top names the dome your parcel is for. Fly through that dome's glowing ring.
Drift
← and → ease off or push ahead.
Pause, music, sound
P pauses, N toggles music, M mutes everything.

Questions

How do I unlock more shifts in Story mode?
Complete a shift to unlock the next one. Earn three stars by finishing without hull damage or missed drops.
Can I reset my progress?
Quit the game and delete it, then reinstall it from the Mac App Store.
Does the game collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
